# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The maker of this app, on an iPad, sitting down to plan the day in front of them. They are not managing a team and not filling a corporate calendar. They want a calmer plan than a grid of fixed appointments, then they hand the day off and leave.

## Product Purpose

Stack a few intention blocks into a timeline and export them to the system Calendar. Success is: the day is assembled, it has real clock times, and it is on the iPad calendar without living inside this app all day.

## Positioning

Flexible routine blocks that assemble from a start time with a named 15-minute buffer, plus one-tap Ripple (+15 minutes, everything after slides). Not a todo list with due dates, not a habit streak, not a calendar of pre-booked slots.

## Operating Context

iPad-first PWA (standalone, safe areas, large taps). Static HTML/CSS/JS, IndexedDB, `.ics` download into iPadOS Calendar. Used as a morning (or anytime) compose-then-export tool, not a live-now dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: stack-from-start-time, 15-minute buffer, Ripple +15, calendar export, local persist, date-stamped day. Preset list and Morning/Afternoon/Evening as currently labeled may be cut or rethought if the day is clearer. Custom-named blocks are not required yet. Do not invent team, billing, or cloud sync.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Daily Intentionality Scheduler (short: Intentional). Binding visual constraints from the owner: do not look AI-generated; **Minecraft-like vibe** (voxel blocks, inventory/hotbar grammar, dirt-stone-grass palette, chunky pixel type, no cream glass, no Caslon timetable). The cream/glass/Caslon UI and the period-timetable sheet are both anti-references. Owner rejected the timetable look.

## Evidence on Hand

Working local app only. No testimonials, screenshots of real usage, or brand assets beyond in-app copy and emoji presets. Future work must not fabricate social proof.

## Product Principles

- Assemble the day; do not perform productivity.
- Clock times are a consequence of stacking, not a second job to set per block.
- One honest handoff to Calendar is enough.
- Cut labels and chrome if they compete with putting a block on the timeline.
