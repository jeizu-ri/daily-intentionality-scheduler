### Overall Impression

The object has a point of view: a calm, analog, windowed day, not a hustle calendar. The first five seconds fight that. The user is asked to pick a “time of day,” an “anchor,” and one of six vault stamps before they have said what they intend. The biggest opportunity is to make **one intention appear on the timeline** in under five seconds, and to make Morning/Afternoon/Evening tell the truth about time.

### What's Working

1. **Time of day as weather.** Accent wash, glass, and card stripe share one hue. That is product atmosphere, not a theme sticker.
2. **Ripple as the hero verb.** `+15 Mins` is the only control whose motion (downstream `.is-shifted`) means something. The 15-minute buffer is named in the timeline subtitle.
3. **Empty-state line is specific.** “Nothing scheduled yet — tap a preset on the left…” is clearer than the rail manifesto.

### Priority Issues

**[P1] First screen asks six things before one intention**
- **Why it matters:** First-timers cannot tell whether Morning, 08:00, or Deep Work is step one. Six equal pills dump choice (cognitive load 6/8 failed; vault is a 6-option decision).
- **Fix:** Default one window. Show 3–4 start blocks; hide the rest. Demote the manifesto. One teaching line: blocks stack from 8:00 AM; Morning/Afternoon/Evening is the *kind* of block, not the hour.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill` then `/impeccable onboard`

**[P1] Time-of-day toggle lies about time**
- **Why it matters:** Evening + Gym still starts at the morning anchor. Color-coding an 8:00 AM “evening” block trains distrust.
- **Fix:** Either the window *is* a start-band (move the anchor and say so), or relabel it “Kind of block” / “Focus” and print that word on the card, not only a 4px stripe.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] High-stakes edits have no seatbelt; the day has no date**
- **Why it matters:** ✕ deletes immediately. `+15` can wrap past midnight with no copy. IndexedDB restores a dateless list as “Today's timeline.”
- **Fix:** Undo toast for remove and ripple; warn on wrap; stamp `localDate` and archive or empty when the calendar day rolls.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Mobile inverts the product**
- **Why it matters:** Under 834px the rail occupies the first viewport; the day and `+15` sit below the fold. `user-scalable=no` blocks zoom. Fine-pointer CSS shrinks taps to 38px.
- **Fix:** Timeline first; compact composer in the thumb zone; restore pinch-zoom; keep 44px targets.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] You cannot author your intention**
- **Why it matters:** A product named Intentionality only offers six canned lives. No rename, duration field, or reorder.
- **Fix:** One Custom block (title + duration); presets as accelerators; up/down or drag order.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape` (narrow: custom block + reorder, not a visual-world replacement)
