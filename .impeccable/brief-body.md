## Audience / job
Owner on iPad. Stamp a few periods, stack from start time with a 15-minute buffer, Ripple +15, export .ics. Presets and windows may be rethought.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day is the hotbar you are holding. One block is selected and huge; the rest wait in numbered slots. It refuses both the cream two-panel dashboard and the printed period timetable.

OWN-WORLD: Dirt brown ground (#3A2A1A), stone slot chrome (#8B8B8B over #5A5A5A), grass-green accent (#5A8C3A), gold for the +15 (#C4A44A). Pixelify Sans is the only face, two sizes. Every box is a 3px hard-edged bevel — light top-left, dark bottom-right — never a shadow or a radius. No blur, no gradient wash, no glass.

STORY: Tap a slot to hold that block; the big held card shows its real clock. +15 grows the held stack and every later block slides. Save & Quit writes the world to Calendar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: iPad landscape. Dirt field edge to edge. Top-left the day's date in the large size; top-right the start-time counter. Center is the held block: name, clock range, duration, +15 and Drop as stone buttons. Under it the green XP-style buffer bar reading "15 min buffer". Bottom is the 9-slot hotbar, filled slots carrying a pixel item and their start clock, the selected one raised by its white pixel frame. Bottom-right stone button: Save & Quit to Calendar. Presets open as an inventory chest grid over the field.

FORM: Hotbar day (my top-ranked grounded candidate, offered as IMPECCABLE'S PICK and locked by the owner). Seed key 0b56577d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Signature interaction
Selecting a slot: the held block swaps with a one-frame, no-ease step (nearest-neighbour feel, 90ms), the slot frame snaps, and the XP bar refills left to right. +15 pushes the later slots down by one pixel step each, staggered 30ms apart, so the ripple is visible as movement down the bar.
